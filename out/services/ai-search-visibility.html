Getting Started

AI search covers tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and similar — where instead of getting a list of links, the user gets a generated answer that names specific businesses. The difference from traditional Google search is that the “result” is a synthesized recommendation, not a ranked page — which means being mentioned at all is the new ranking.
